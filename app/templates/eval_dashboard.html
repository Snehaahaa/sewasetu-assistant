Evals: invented people, real client, real database

Each person below is invented (name, age, village, how she talks, what she knows, what she wants). Each is played against the assistant through a real MCP client; afterwards we read this portal's Postgres and check what is actually there. The database decides pass or fail; what the assistant said is shown, and checked loosely, but never decides. Everything here is synthetic (test mobiles 99001… / 99002…).
